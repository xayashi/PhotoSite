The leaves have turned, painting the landscape in warm hues of amber and gold. There's something magical about this time of year—when the world seems to slow down, take a breath, and prepare for the stillness of winter.

![Morning light through the trees](/images/portrait.jpg)

I spent a weekend exploring the trails near home, camera in hand, searching for moments worth capturing. The early morning light was particularly beautiful, filtering through the remaining leaves and casting long shadows across the path.

> "Autumn is a second spring when every leaf is a flower." — Albert Camus

The colors were extraordinary this year. Perhaps it was the late summer rains, or maybe I was simply paying closer attention. Either way, I found myself stopping every few steps, mesmerized by the display.

## A Moment of Reflection

Photography has taught me to slow down. To really *see* what's in front of me. In our rush to get somewhere, we often miss the beauty that surrounds us. These quiet mornings in the woods have become a kind of meditation.

The crunch of leaves underfoot. The crisp morning air. The way the sunlight dances through the canopy. These are the moments I live for.

---

Tomorrow brings more adventures. Until then, I'll sit with these images and remember the peace of that morning walk.
