We drove for hours into the darkness, following forecasts and intuition. The road narrowed until it was just two tire tracks in the snow. Then we stopped, turned off the headlights, and waited.

Nothing prepares you for the first time. The sky doesn't just glow. It moves. It breathes. Ribbons of green light folded and unfolded overhead, silent and impossibly vast.

## The Waiting Game

Most of this trip was waiting. Sitting in the cold, watching clear skies turn cloudy, driving to the next gap in the weather. Photography at the edge of the world is mostly patience.

But when the sky opens up, none of that matters.

## Silence

What struck me most wasn't the color. It was the silence. Standing alone in a frozen field, watching the atmosphere catch fire, hearing absolutely nothing. The experience lives somewhere beyond what a photograph can hold.

I took these images knowing they'd fall short. But they're enough to remember the feeling.
