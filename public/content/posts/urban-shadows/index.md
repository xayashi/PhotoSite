There's a moment just after sunset in Shinjuku when the neon hasn't fully taken over. The sky holds its last breath of deep blue, and the alleyways become corridors of contrast. I found myself drawn to these in-between spaces.

## The Space Between

Every shadow is cast by something luminous. Walking through the narrow streets, I started seeing the city not as a place of overwhelming brightness, but as an intricate dance between what is lit and what is hidden.

> "In the right light, at the right time, everything is extraordinary."

The rain had just stopped. Puddles became mirrors, doubling the city beneath my feet. I crouched low, letting the reflections fill the frame.

## Neon Veins

By midnight, the streets pulse with a rhythm of their own. Salarymen shuffle past, their silhouettes cutting through curtains of colored light. I shot this entire series handheld, letting the motion blur become part of the story.

The imperfection felt honest.

## Looking Back

Tokyo taught me that beauty doesn't ask for your attention. It simply exists, layered beneath the noise, waiting for someone to stop and look.

---

I'll return to these streets. There are more shadows to find.
